Why we built this

GestureCalc bridges the gap between human-computer interaction and accessibility. By translating American Sign Language (ASL) digits and operator gestures into real-time mathematical expressions, we are creating a hands-free, intuitive, and inclusive way to compute — for everyone.

When you first launch the app, a short interactive calibration wizard will guide you through optimal lighting, hand distance, and a single gesture test to ensure accurate recognition.

Gesture Guide

Hold each gesture steady for 1 second to commit it. Single-hand gestures use finger counting. Digits 6–10 combine two hands.

Single-Hand Digits (0–5)

✊0Closed fist
☝️1Index finger up
✌️2V-sign (horizontal)
🤘3Index + pinky out
🖐⬇👍44 fingers, no thumb
🖐5Open hand, all fingers

Two-Hand Digits (6–10)

Show both hands — finger counts are added together.

☝️+🖐61 + 5 fingers
✌️+🖐72 + 5 fingers
🤘+🖐83 + 5 fingers
🖐+495 + 4 fingers
🖐+🖐105 + 5 fingers

Operators (Inverted Hand — Palm Down)

👍+Thumbs up
👎−Thumbs down
🤘⬇×Rock sign, inverted
✌️⬇÷V-sign, inverted
👇=Point down (evaluate)

Actions

🖐⬇ClearOpen hand, inverted
✊⬇BackFist, inverted
